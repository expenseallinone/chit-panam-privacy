Chit Panam — Account & Data Deletion

Developer: Collection Diary

Chit Panam is a digital ledger for recording chit members, instalments and collections. It does not provide loans or lending services.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to expenseallinone@gmail.com with the subject Chit Panam Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the mobile number or account identifier linked to your Chit Panam account. Do not send your password or OTP.
  3. State whether you request deletion of your account and associated data. Our support team may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

For assistance, contact +91 7907165919.

Data covered by your request

The request covers your Chit Panam account/profile information and associated digital-ledger data, including member records, instalment and collection entries that the service stores on your behalf, subject to lawful retention requirements and the rights of other account holders.

Data that may be retained

Some transaction records, security logs, dispute-related records or other information may need to be retained where required by applicable law, to prevent fraud or to protect the rights of other people. We will explain any data retained and applicable retention period when responding to your verified request.

Important: This page provides a way to request deletion; it does not automatically delete an account. The developer must operate a working process to review and complete eligible requests. A fixed deletion timeframe has not yet been specified; contact support for the applicable processing and retention periods.
