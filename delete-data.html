Chit Panam – Request Data Deletion

Developer: Collection Diary

Chit Panam is a digital ledger application used to manage chit-related records, members, instalments and collection entries. It does not provide loans or lending services.

You can request deletion of selected data without deleting your Chit Panam account.

How to request deletion

  1. Email expenseallinone@gmail.com with the subject Chit Panam – Data Deletion Request.
  2. Identify your account using your registered mobile number or account identifier. Do not send your password or OTP.
  3. Describe the specific member records, chit records, instalment entries, or collection entries you want removed.
  4. Our support team will verify account ownership and respond with the next steps. You may keep your account while requesting deletion of selected records.

Which data is deleted?

Once a deletion request is verified and approved, the identified records will be removed from the application's server database, subject to applicable legal and operational retention requirements. Related records may need to be handled together to preserve ledger consistency. We will explain any records that cannot be deleted.

Which data may be kept?

Account information not included in the request may remain so you can continue using the application. Records required for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or other legitimate retention obligations may be retained only as necessary. The exact retention period depends on the applicable requirement; contact us for information about your request.

Note: Removing a record from the app screen is not by itself proof that it has been deleted from the server. You can request confirmation after processing.
